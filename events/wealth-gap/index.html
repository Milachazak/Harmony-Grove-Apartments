The five lessons

Every good hospital runs on protocols.
Your money needs one too.

Derick calls it a Financial Operating System. It has five parts. He will walk you through each one. Here is what you will learn. And why it matters to you.

01
Tax Strategy

Keep more of what you earn

Most people think about taxes in April. By then the year is over. Derick shows how to plan all year long. You will hear the moves high earners use. Tax smart investments. Faster depreciation. Smarter giving. The right entity setup. Credits and incentives. You will also learn the difference between a tax recorder and a tax strategist.

Why it matters to you

Tax is often the biggest bill a high earner pays. A dollar you legally keep is a sure thing. Derick calls it the only guaranteed return there is.

02
Growth & Cash Flow

Make your money pay you

Net worth is a score. Income is what buys your freedom. Derick explains how professional investors think. They weigh five levers. Growth. Safety. Access to cash. Taxes. Control. The real question is not how much you have. It is how much income your money creates.

Why it matters to you

Your paycheck needs you at work. Income from assets does not. That is how you buy back your time.

03
Systems & Automation

Put your wealth on autopilot

Willpower fades in a busy month. Automation does not. Derick shows a simple money flow. Every paycheck feeds your tax fund first. Then your savings. Then your investments. It all happens before you can spend it. Do nothing special and your goals still get funded.

Why it matters to you

Long shifts and call nights leave no time to move money by hand. A system works while you work.

04
Protection & Safeguards

Protect what you have built

What if you get sick or hurt? What if a partner leaves the practice? What if a key person quits? Derick walks through the curveballs people forget to plan for. You will see how the right reserves and coverage keep one bad year from undoing ten good ones.

Why it matters to you

Your ability to practice is your biggest asset. Your family depends on it. It deserves a real plan.

05
Business Independence

Build income that does not need you

Derick has a saying. The business that needs you owns you. He shows how buyers value a business. He says the people and systems behind it can drive up to 70% of its value. Then he shows how to build one that runs without you.

Why it matters to you

Could you step away for 30 days? If the answer is no then your income is tied to your time. Own a practice? Learn to build one worth selling. Employed? Learn to build income outside your paycheck.
