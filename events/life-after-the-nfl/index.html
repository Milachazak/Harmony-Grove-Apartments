The idea behind the night

Every career ends one day. The question is what keeps paying you after.

A football career can end on one play. By age 26 Kenny had suffered eight football injuries. A medical career starts late and runs on long hours. Both leave fewer years to build wealth. So we asked a Super Bowl champion how he plans for life after the game.

The conversation

Five topics.
One honest conversation.

This is not a lecture. Dr. Kirk Campbell will interview Kenny like a podcast. A few slides will show his journey. Here is what they will cover. And why it matters to you.

01
The Journey

From future surgeon to the NFL

Kenny studied anatomy at Duke. He planned to become a heart surgeon. Then football called. He made the Denver Broncos as an undrafted free agent. He will share how he chose between the two. And what it took to earn his spot.

Why it matters to you

Kenny nearly took the road you took. He knows long training and hard choices. His story will feel familiar.

02
Money Lessons

What the NFL taught him about money

Pro football can pay well for a short time. Big paychecks bring big pressure. Kenny will share the money lessons he learned in the league. He will also share what he wishes he had known on day one.

Why it matters to you

A high income draws the same attention to doctors. Requests. Pitches. Deals. The lessons carry over.

03
Building Together

How he and his wife got started

After the NFL Kenny and his wife began building a real estate portfolio. He will talk about how they started. And how they make money decisions as a team.

Why it matters to you

Your spouse is part of your plan. Wealth is built by two people pulling in the same direction. Bring them to the call.

04
Why Multifamily

Why he chose apartments

Kenny had many choices for his money. He chose multifamily real estate. He will explain why. One property holds many homes. So it collects many rents. And everyone needs a place to live.

Why it matters to you

You do not have time to be a landlord. Many professionals own apartments through an experienced operator. They own a share. The operator does the work.

05
Value-Add Real Estate

Cash flow, growth and legacy

Value-add means buying a property that can be made better. Better homes and better management can raise its value over time. Kenny will explain why he likes this approach. He will cover cash flow and growth. And the legacy it can leave for his family.

Why it matters to you

Your paycheck stops when you stop. Assets can keep paying you. They can also pass to the next generation.

Kenny Anunike

Super Bowl 50 Champion · Former NFL Defensive End

Kenny made the Denver Broncos in 2014 as an undrafted free agent. He played defensive end and wore number 91. He was on the Broncos team that won Super Bowl 50.

Before the NFL he starred at Duke. He was named All-ACC. He left as the school's career sacks leader. He earned a degree in biological anthropology and anatomy. He was studying to become a heart surgeon.

After the NFL he coached at Ohio State and Fordham. At Ohio State he worked with future NFL stars Chase Young and Nick Bosa. He also earned master's degrees at Duke and Ohio State. Today he and his wife are building a real estate portfolio of their own.

He played at the highest level. Now he plays a longer game.
