01
Masterclass

Underwriting Masters

Masterclass, 60 to 90 min, or a multi week series

The series Claude teaches live, condensed for your audience. Rent rolls, trailing twelve statements, comps, NOI and cap rates, debt and sensitivity tables, in the order a lender reads them. A model that balances can still be completely wrong; this is how to find out before you invest.

  • Read a rent roll and a T-12 with confidence
  • Build the downside case before the base case
  • Spot the one assumption carrying the deal
02
Keynote

From Immigrant to Owner

Keynote, 30 to 45 min

From selling fruit at a bus station in western Cameroon, to learning English while volunteering in Johannesburg, to a corporate finance career and the closing table in Atlanta. A story about discipline with every dollar, and building wealth on purpose.

  • Why every dollar matters before you have many
  • Building an investing business alongside a career
  • Ownership as a path for immigrant and diaspora families
03
Keynote or panel

Stop Buying Another Job

Keynote or panel, 30 to 45 min

Claude's first two single family rentals taught him he was buying himself another nine to five. Apartments are different: buying an apartment building is buying a business, valued on its income, with a plan and an exit.

  • Single family versus multifamily, honestly compared
  • How income, not comps, sets an apartment's value
  • Why the business plan and the exit come first
04
Workshop

Hire the Operator, Not the Building

Workshop, 60 to 90 min

For passive investors: when you invest in a syndication you are hiring someone to manage your money, and the team is the biggest risk in the deal. Claude shows how to vet a sponsor before you wire a dollar.

  • The questions that reveal an operator's experience
  • Reading alignment: who invests alongside you
  • What good investor reporting looks like

About

Every dollar counted, every number defended.

Raised in a town of fewer than 2,000 people in western Cameroon, J. Claude Mouaffi sold fruit at the bus station after school. French was his first language; he learned English while volunteering with children in Johannesburg. He studied accounting and finance in Cameroon and South Africa and built a career in corporate finance, first in telecom infrastructure and then in the United States.

The BiggerPockets podcast pulled him into real estate. He bought two single family homes, realized he was buying himself another job, and moved to apartment syndications, learning the business from experienced mentors. He is principal of Chazak Investment in Maryland and a founding principal of Mila Penn Chazak, where he leads deal sourcing, underwriting and capital structuring. In 2026 he helped close two metro Atlanta acquisitions, including a 75 unit Marietta community funded in 65 days.

Underwriting is the work he is known for, and he still does it himself. After four years spent coaching people through their own finances, he now mentors new underwriters in Underwriting Masters, a free live series capped at 15 seats, and in every investor session he walks through rent rolls, budgets and downside cases line by line.
